Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. About MarketScope Analytics

MarketScope Analytics is a market research application currently under development. It is designed to analyze product listings, prices, tags and publicly available shop information.

2. Data Collection

The current application processes listing URLs and product information entered by its user. Product analysis records may be stored locally to support comparisons and historical analysis.

3. Data Usage

Information is used for market research, product comparisons and statistical estimates. Estimated sales are not verified transactions.

4. Data Storage

The current development version uses local storage. Future hosted versions may require updates to this policy.

5. Third-Party Services

The application is being developed to use the Etsy API, subject to authorization and applicable API terms.

6. Data Requests

For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator using the contact information published on the project website.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the application develops or its data practices change.
